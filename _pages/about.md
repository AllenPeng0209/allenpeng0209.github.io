I am an autonomous-driving researcher and engineer working at the intersection of **robotics, multimodal foundation models, and embodied intelligence**. My work focuses on building learning-based driving systems that connect large-scale data, 3D scene understanding, planning, and deployment on production vehicles.

I currently work on end-to-end autonomous driving and multimodal models at **Great Wall Motor**. Previously, I led occupancy perception and production deployment projects at Horizon Robotics, developed vehicle-side perception systems at QCraft, and worked on multi-task LiDAR perception at DeepRoute.ai.

My research interests include:

- Vision-language-action models and multimodal learning for autonomous driving
- End-to-end driving, behavior modeling, and closed-loop evaluation
- 3D perception, occupancy prediction, and neural scene reconstruction
- Efficient model training and deployment for real-world robotic systems

I received master's degrees in Computer Science and Technology from **Tsinghua University** and in Data Science from the **University of California, Berkeley**. I received my bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering, with a minor in Computer Science, from **National Cheng Kung University**.

I am interested in collaborations that bridge research ideas with robust, deployable autonomous systems.

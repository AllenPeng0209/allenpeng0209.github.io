Built a large-scale training pipeline for multimodal end-to-end driving models, shortening the path from raw recorded data to model training and supporting navigation, images, and point clouds as inputs.

The architecture work extended a vision-language model for long temporal context and introduced a trajectory-distribution and control-output head. The resulting system completed an end-to-end vehicle demonstration with navigation-conditioned turning behavior.

**Focus:** vision-language-action models, large-scale data, temporal modeling, trajectory prediction, control.

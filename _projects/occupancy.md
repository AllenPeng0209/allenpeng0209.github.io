Led the development of an automotive occupancy perception stack for urban navigation-on-autopilot, covering data collection, full-scene 3D ground-truth production, model training, onboard deployment, and closed-loop issue resolution.

The project brought a new vehicle-side model from initial prototype to production readiness and coordinated requirements across labeling, perception, planning, and control. It was delivered on the Horizon Journey 6 platform for multiple automotive programs.

**Focus:** occupancy prediction, 3D perception, automotive deployment, cross-functional technical leadership.
